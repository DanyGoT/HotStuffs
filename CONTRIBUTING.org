* Naming conventions

Use common sense, and follow these where they apply:

- Commits: =type(scope): description=, in lowercase and the imperative, with
  no body. Types: =feat=, =fix=, =docs=, =refactor=, =chore=, =build=, =perf=.
- Branches: =type/[issue_no-]description=, e.g. =feature/32-repository-hygiene=
  or =refactor/proto-views=. Use =feature= for =feat=.

Run =make test=.
